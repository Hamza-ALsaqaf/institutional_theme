Designed to meet WCAG 2.2 AA; formal audit recommended. Independent reviewer, did not write the code. Scope: `git diff origin/main..HEAD -- . ':!docs/team/evidence'` (branch head `8a0480f`; the user-facing change is `institutional_theme/public/scss/ins_print.bundle.scss`, commits `4d7dba3` and `0467b4e`), the evidence in `docs/team/evidence/round6/hrms-print/`, `README.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`. The desk CSS headroom change is not reviewed here (separate addendum). The Python cache logic, tests and tooling are outside my dimensions.

Labels. VERIFIED = I ran it or opened the PDF or PNG myself. COMPUTED = from a file or from a pixel I sampled in a rendered PDF, with the WCAG formula. ASSUMED = not run.

Method (VERIFIED). I did not re-render anything (the benches are not mine and `/home/frappe` was not touched). I used the 80 `themed__*.pdf` files in `v16.32.0/` and `v16.52.0/`, rendered them with `pdftoppm` into `/tmp/claude-a11y/r6/` (60 dpi montages en | ar for all formats, 100 to 600 dpi crops where I needed detail), read `pdffonts`, `pdfinfo`, `pdftotext -bbox` and `-raw` on them, sampled pixels with Pillow, and read the HRMS v16.50.0 print-format JSON (`/home/user/src/hrms-16.50.0/hrms/payroll/print_format/salary_slip_*/`) for the CSS the theme overrides. Viewed in Arabic and English, both generators where the format allows: Salary Slip Classic, Detailed, Compact (16.52.0 only; on 16.52.0 a wkhtmltopdf request returns Chrome output, as the team says), Salary Slip Standard (HRMS on 16.32.0; core's auto layout on 16.52.0), Salary Slip with Year to Date and based on Timesheet (16.32.0), Leave Application, Expense Claim, Job Offer, Appointment Letter. I opened Employee and Attendance only through the font and margin scans, not visually.

## 1. Findings

Severity. BLOCKING = a user of the supported configuration cannot read or use something that matters, or a claim is false in a way that changes the gate. MAJOR = real and measurable, the document can still be read, or the cause is core or HRMS but the product ships it without saying so. MINOR = observation, cosmetic, or a documented limit.

**No BLOCKING finding.**

### MAJOR

**1. Grey label and sub-text on the tinted bands of the builder slips is 4.12:1 and 4.26:1, below the 4.5:1 the BRIEF requires on tinted backgrounds (Classic, Detailed, Compact; 16.52.0; en and ar).**
- Where: HRMS CSS (`.ss-label`, `.ss-net .words`, `.ss-hero .words`, `.ss-strip .ss-label`, `.ss-ledger tr.ss-sec td` all `color: #757575` at 9.5 to 10.5 px, bands `.ss-net` and `.ss-hero` `#f2f2f2`, `.ss-strip` `#f6f6f6`). The theme overrides these slips' font family (`ins_print.bundle.scss:28-33`) and their sides (`:441-510`) but not their colours. The theme's own secondary text colour (`$ins-text-secondary` `#424b57`) would give 7.90 and 8.18.
- Measured (COMPUTED from pixels of the rendered PDFs): `z-classic-ar-net` crop of `themed__salary-slip-salary-slip-classic__chrome-ar.pdf`: ink `rgb(117,117,117)` on band `rgb(242,242,242)`, 4.12:1; `themed__salary-slip-salary-slip-detailed__chrome-en.pdf` strip: ink `rgb(117,117,117)` on `rgb(246,246,246)`, 4.26:1 (the four "working days / payment days / leave / absent" captions). The same grey on white is 4.61:1.
- User experience: the "Net pay" caption, the amount in words ("SAR Fifteen Thousand ...", "Net paid year to date") and the day-count captions are the hardest to read line of the slip, at 7.1 to 7.9 pt, tracked, grey on grey. On a black-and-white photocopy (BRIEF 8, Print) they are the first text to drop out. The amount itself (`#1a1a1a`, 15.55:1) is fine.
- Fix: one rule inside `.print-format` mapping those selectors to `$ins-text-secondary`, and raise the 9.5 px captions to 10 px or more. No `!important` needed (the theme prefix adds specificity).

**2. Arabic text in the builder slips is letter-spaced (BRIEF 8: "Arabic: no letter-spacing"; the theme's own comment at `ins_print.bundle.scss:416`: "tracking breaks the word apart").**
- Where: HRMS `.ss-label { letter-spacing: 0.1em }`, `.ss-ledger th { 0.06em }`, `.ss-ledger tr.ss-sec td { 0.1em }`, `.ss-title h3 { 0.1em }`. The theme resets `letter-spacing` only for the watermark (`:417-419`); the new RTL block (`:441-510`) mirrors sides but leaves tracking.
- Observed (VERIFIED): `themed__salary-slip-salary-slip-classic__chrome-ar.pdf` at 600 dpi, "صافي الراتب" (net pay): the joins are kept but every letter pair is pulled apart ("صا في ال را تب"); the same on the column headers "كمية", "المستحقات", "استقطاعات" and in Compact on "PAYSLIP FOR" next to Arabic. `pdftotext -layout` of the same file returns the headers as "ك مية" and "ص ايف ا لرا ت ب", which is how the tracking shows to a text consumer.
- User experience: the Arabic captions look broken or misspelt compared with the body; they are legible, but this is the Arabic-first product's most visible print page.
- Fix: under `[dir="rtl"] .print-format`, `.ss-label, .ss-ledger th, .ss-ledger tr.ss-sec td, .ss-title h3 { letter-spacing: 0 }`.

**3. A date that follows an Arabic word is drawn with its segments reversed, so one line shows two date formats (all three Arabic slips, 16.52.0).**
- Where: HRMS markup `{{ _("For the period") }} {{ frappe.format(doc.start_date) }} {{ _("to") }} {{ frappe.format(doc.end_date) }}` and `{{ _("Issued") }} {{ frappe.format(doc.posting_date) }}`, no `<bdi>` or `dir` on the date. The theme cannot isolate a text node from CSS.
- Observed (VERIFIED, Classic, Detailed and Compact Arabic PDFs): the period line reads, left to right, `2026-09-30 إلى For the period 01-09-2026` (Classic) and `2026-09-30 إلى 01-09-2026` (Detailed, Compact); "نشر 2026-09-30" for the issue date. The site's date format is `dd-mm-yyyy` (the English PDFs print "01-09-2026 to 30-09-2026"). The start date is right; the end date, which follows "إلى", is shown as `yyyy-mm-dd`.
- Why (COMPUTED from the Unicode bidi rules, not from the renderer): the digits after an Arabic letter become Arabic numbers (rule W2); the hyphen is a European separator, which rule W4 only joins between European numbers, so each segment is reordered. A slash is a common separator (W4 joins it), so `dd/mm/yyyy` would not reverse; I did not render that.
- User experience: with `dd-mm-yyyy` the reversed form is a valid ISO date, so no value is wrong, but the two ends of one period use two formats on the headline of the primary HR document. With a site format of `mm-dd-yyyy` (Frappe's default for US sites) the reversed form would read `yyyy-dd-mm` (COMPUTED, not rendered), which is wrong for a reader who takes it as ISO. Amounts are not affected (the comma and the period are common separators).
- Not fixable in the theme. Options: an HRMS patch (`<bdi>` around the dates), a site date format with slashes, or a documented limitation. It is not documented in README, CHANGELOG or the round-6 README.

**4. Salary Slip "Standard" on Frappe v16.52.0 loses data at the page edge in Chrome, and overlaps headers in wkhtmltopdf (core layout, undisclosed to users).**
- Where: core's auto-generated Standard (HRMS v16.50.0 dropped its own Standard, Year to Date and Timesheet formats, so this is what the Salary Slip prints in unless the user picks a builder format). Team README defect 6 describes it; `CHANGELOG.md` "Known limitations" and `README.md` "Printing" do not.
- Observed (VERIFIED, `themed__salary-slip-standard__chrome-ar.pdf` at 100 dpi and the montages): two six-column tables side by side in half-width columns. Chrome English clips the right table's last column ("Tax on additional s…"); Chrome Arabic clips the left table's last column at the left page edge (`ر.س` cut to `ر.` at x = 43 px of 827, header "الضريبة على الراتب الإضافي" collides with the "ر.ت" serial header); wkhtmltopdf fits both tables but the headers overprint ("Tax on additional salary" over "Sr"; Arabic "الضريبة على الراتب الإضافي" over "مكون"). The team shows the same without the theme.
- User experience: a deduction column of a pay slip prints cut off, with no warning. In the seed the cut column is 0.00, so a real slip with a value there would lose it.
- Mitigation exists (Classic, Detailed, Compact fit and are now correct in Arabic) but it is advice in an evidence README, not in the product documentation.

**5. The wkhtmltopdf Arabic PDFs have an unusable text layer, and all 80 PDFs are untagged with no language (platform; README says both generators are supported).**
- Observed (VERIFIED): `pdftotext -raw themed__leave-application-standard__wkhtmltopdf-ar.pdf` returns single presentation-form glyphs on separate lines (`ا`, `ﳌ`, `ﻮ`, `ﻇ`, `ﻒ` for "الموظف"); the Chrome PDF of the same page returns "الموظف" joined (with the odd letter swaps that pdftotext shows on Chrome Arabic, for example "رشكة" for "شركة", which may be my tool, not the file). So copy, search and a screen reader would not get the Arabic words from a wkhtmltopdf PDF. All 80 themed PDFs: `Tagged: no`, no `/StructTreeRoot`, no `/Lang` (byte search of one file, `pdfinfo` of all), producer `pypdf` (core merges the header page and the body page); 8 of 80 have a Title and it is the applicant's name (Job Offer, Appointment Letter). So no heading, no table-header semantics and no document language survive in any PDF, in either generator, while the HTML source of Classic and Detailed does have `<h3>` and `<th>` (Compact has none).
- Cause not in this app (core's merge and the generator). Disclosure is missing: README line 88 says "both are supported".
- Not tested: whether Acrobat, a browser PDF viewer or a screen reader reads the Chrome PDFs correctly.

### MINOR

**6. Watermark position, per generator (the round's main change). What the PDFs show against what the docs claim.**
- wkhtmltopdf (`top: 118mm`, `ins_print.bundle.scss:361-364`): Leave Application (en, ar) sits over empty space as the README says. Expense Claim, 16.32.0 and 16.52.0 (en, ar): the box starts just under the second item row and its frame and letters cross the wrapped last line of that row ("- TI") and the first two totals blocks ("Total Sanctioned Amount (Company Currency) 1,720.00", "Total Advance Amount ... 0.00"; Arabic "اجمالي الكمية الموافق عليه", "مقدما"). Text over the 12% ink is still 13.5:1 (COMPUTED: ink `rgb(226,227,228)`, text `#141b25`, 16.34 against black; the muted `#757575` would be 3.59 on it, but no muted text sits under the letters in these samples). `README.md` (round-6 evidence, defect 3) says "over empty space on a short document" and `CHANGELOG.md` says it "no longer lands on the table rows": true for Leave Application, only partly true for Expense Claim.
- Chrome, `top: 10mm` inside `@supports (display: grid)` (`:387-391`): the box sits over the letter head and the title block on every document (disclosed). On 16.32.0 the frame's bottom border is not printed: my scan of `themed__leave-application-standard__chrome-en.pdf` and `themed__expense-claim-standard__chrome-ar.pdf` at 110 dpi finds the top border (6 px) and the two sides but no bottom border; on 16.52.0 (`themed__expense-claim-standard__chrome-en.pdf`, `themed__leave-application-standard__chrome-ar.pdf`) the bottom border is there. The manifest row and the SCSS comment say a box at 10 mm "is whole"; on 16.32.0 it is not. The border is the part that survives a photocopy, so the mark there is an open "U" of lines around the word.
- Not tested: a multi-page draft (the SCSS comment says a fixed box repeats on every page in both generators; the team did not run it), the Cancelled (dashed) variant, no-letter-head prints in Chrome, Letter paper.

**7. Mixed-direction English strings in Arabic prints keep their punctuation on the wrong end.** Job Offer and Appointment Letter (both versions, both generators): ",Dear Rana Jaber", ".We are pleased to appoint you ...", ",Yours truly" and ".Probation: The first three months ... period" (the full stop drawn before the sentence); Year to Date slips: ":Year To Date", ":Month To Date", ".Hundred And Ninety Five only". The theme's existing `unicode-bidi: plaintext` fix is scoped to `div.info-text` (`:404-407`, Quotation labels) and wkhtmltopdf ignores it. Cause: the HRMS strings are not translated in the seed, so English runs sit in an RTL paragraph; real translated letters would not show it. Suggestion for Chrome: extend `unicode-bidi: plaintext` to `p, li` and the label cells under `[dir="rtl"]`.

**8. A date wraps inside its own digits in narrow columns.** Expense Claim item rows (both languages, both generators): "11-10-" then "2026" on the next line. Core table width; not theme-caused; reads as two numbers.

**9. Core and HRMS limits that are disclosed in the evidence README and not in the user-facing docs:** Chrome drops the document title and number on 16.32.0 Salary Slip Standard, Leave Application and Expense Claim (the 16.32.0 Chrome Salary Slip prints no heading and no slip number: `themed__salary-slip-salary-slip-standard__chrome-en.pdf`, the wkhtmltopdf one prints "Sal Slip/HR-EMP-00003/00001"); the letter-head rule is about 3.5 mm wider than the content on the end side (in Arabic the content starts about 4 mm inside the rule on the right: `52-classic-ar`); Standard Appointment Letter prints without the letter head and with the literal text `Dear {{ applicant_name }},` (the seed's template text, `tools/bench/hrms_print_seed.py:318`, so a seed artefact, not a theme defect; the PDF still carries it).

**10. Physical properties in the new RTL block.** `ins_print.bundle.scss:441-510` uses `text-align: left/right`, `padding-left/right`, `border-left/right`, against `CLAUDE.md` "logical properties only". The file header explains the exception for wkhtmltopdf's old WebKit; the comment on the block says these formats are Chrome-only, which would allow `text-align: end` and `*-inline-*`. Not wrong (the physical pair works in both generators and is inside `rtl:ignore`); the stated reason and the practice do not match. No change required if the lead accepts the exception.

**11. Documentation.** `README.md:5` still opens with "Status: Round 4 ... passed its release gate" under a README that now says it describes release 1.0.0. `CHANGELOG.md` "Known limitations" lists screen readers and Windows High Contrast and says "a formal accessibility audit is still required before production" (good); it does not list findings 3, 4, 5 or the Chrome title loss. ## 2. What I checked, with values

**Font embedding (VERIFIED, `pdffonts` on all 80 themed PDFs).** Every font is embedded (`emb yes` on all rows); the only families are IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Sans Arabic and their Medium or SemiBold cuts; no Liberation, DejaVu, Arial or Helvetica in any themed PDF. The three builder formats on 16.52.0 print Plex in both languages. The `noapp__salary-slip-salary-slip-classic__chrome-*.pdf` files embed `DejaVuSans LiberationSans LiberationSans-Bold`, which confirms the before state the team describes. The README's font claim holds. (The CSS private family name is not visible in a PDF; I did not check `ship.py`.)

**Mirroring (VERIFIED by viewing, Classic, Detailed and Compact, Arabic, Chrome).** Component names at the right edge, amounts at the left; column order runs right to left (Detailed: component, this period, year to date from the right; Standard: serial first at the right); the earnings table is on the right and deductions on the left; the net-pay bar is on the right edge with the caption at the right and the figure at the left; the Detailed strip separators and the Compact footer run right to left; section rules sit at the inline end of the section title; the header block metadata columns mirror (Classic labels right, values left). English is unchanged (Classic en viewed). Amounts are not reversed digit-wise ("11,100.00", "15,595.00", "14.50"), and the currency sign sits after the number in both languages ("11,100.00 ر.س"). Not exercised by the seed: `.ss-hero .pay` (bank details), `.ss-timesheets`, a deduction-less slip.

**Mixed strings.** Findings 3 and 7. The Arabic names in English slips join correctly ("سارة المنصور"); an Arabic component name in an English slip is not exercised in the evidence I opened.

**Tinted bands.** Classic and Compact net band `#f2f2f2` (pixel `rgb(242,242,242)`), Detailed strip `#f6f6f6`; body text `#1a1a1a` 15.55:1; captions finding 1. The theme's own print colours (`$ins-text-strong #141b25`, `$ins-text-secondary #424b57`) on white are above 9:1; the theme adds no tinted band of its own in this diff.

**Clipping at the A4 margins (VERIFIED).** Text-layer scan of all 80 themed PDFs (`pdftotext -bbox`): no word starts or ends within 8 mm of a page edge, on any page. That does not catch clipping by an inner container: finding 4 is a column cut by the table's cell, seen only in the raster.

**Watermark.** Finding 6. Measured: ink `rgb(226,227,228)` (12% of `#141b25`), frame 6 px, `top` as above; the team's `watermark-measure/wm2.png` and `wm3.png` (viewed) show the Chrome box at 10 mm whole, cut lower and absent further down, which is the reason for the two values; I did not re-run the measurement.

**Headings and table headers.** Finding 5: none survive in a PDF. HTML source (HRMS v16.50.0 JSON): Classic 9 `<th>` in 3 `<thead>` and one `<h3>`; Detailed 15 `<th>`, 4 `<thead>`, one `<h3>`; Compact none and no heading (section captions are `<td>` with a class). No `scope`, no `<caption>`, no `dir` on any of them.

**Not applicable to print.** Focus, keyboard order, sticky columns, scroll cues, forced colours, reduced motion, zoom and coarse pointer do not apply to the PDFs; the Print Style is not changed in those respects by this diff.

**Fidelity (listed, not judged).** `design/canvas/Print.dc.html` (9.5 KB) contains no watermark and no HRMS slip; there is no approved artboard for the salary-slip formats or the watermark position, so the result cannot be matched to one. The watermark is a framed, 96 px, 12%-ink word per the existing SCSS; in wkhtmltopdf it sits at 118 mm, in Chrome at 10 mm, so the same document carries the mark in two places depending on the generator.
