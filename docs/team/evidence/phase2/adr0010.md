## Colour-vision baseline

`institutional_theme/audits/data/colour_vision_baseline.json`, written by `python -m institutional_theme.audits.colour_vision --write-baseline` for the five shipped presets (21, 19, 21, 19, 19 gaps; 99 in total). The audit now fails (exit 1) on: a failing status pair, a failing neighbour pair of series 1 to 6 (6 beside 1) in any shipped preset, mode and vision (including grayscale), and any other pair of series 1 to 8 below 5.0 that is not in the baseline or is worse than its baseline by more than 0.1. Tests: `institutional_theme/audits/tests/test_colour_vision.py` (a gap removed from the baseline is fatal, a gap that got worse is fatal, no baseline means every gap is new, every neighbour passes, baseline equals the measurement).

The midad baseline reproduces the ADR's accepted gaps: grayscale chart-2/5 0.3 (Light); chart-4/6 0.3, chart-2/6 0.6, chart-2/4 1.0 (Dark); protanopia chart-3/6 4.4 (Dark); series 7 and 8 down to 0.3 (grayscale).

Neighbours: every shipped preset passes 5.0 in all visions and modes (`fixround/audits.log`, "Neighbouring series 1-6").
